# Landing page (signed-out "/")

Mode: Persuade. Audience: adult children of elders with early memory loss, doctors, care homes in India, arriving from a shared link or search. Job: understand what Khayal does and for whom, trust that it is honest and private, and request an invite. Proof on hand: the working product itself (patient Home, games, caregiver trends), the method (adaptive difficulty, personal-baseline decline tracking) and its simulated evaluation. No testimonials, clinical claims or endorsements exist; none may be invented. Pinned by the brief: palette deep teal + saffron + warm ivory, logo idea, section list, motion rules, Atkinson Hyperlegible as the brand face.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Khayal is a home, not a clinic or an app store tile. The page is a walk through an Indian courtyard house; it refuses the health-tech template of a white page, a gradient hero and rows of equal icon cards.

OWN-WORLD: lime-plaster ivory walls; deep teal as painted wood that owns whole regions (hero door, final call to action, footer); saffron as the marigold garland and the one action colour; jaali lattice light, drawn from the logo mark, falling across teal; arched niches frame the product; Atkinson Hyperlegible at full display scale.

STORY: a family member sees what their parent's day would look like, how they would know if something changes, that it is honest (not a medical device) and private (stays in India), then asks for an invite.

FIRST VIEWPORT: a sticky plaster lintel header (mark, links, Sign in, saffron Request an invite). Below, a teal door field: headline at display scale left, subline, saffron primary and quiet secondary; at right, an arched niche holding the patient Home screen on a phone, jaali light falling across it. On phones the niche sits under the copy, still above the fold in part.

FORM: Courtyard home with jaali screens and painted teal doors, candidate 6 of 7. seed:092ea93211d4ba62

SIGNATURE INTERACTION: the hero mark draws itself in once; the jaali light shifts slowly as you scroll the hero (off under reduced motion). Rooms (sections) fade up as you reach them, each with its own small entrance, 150 to 400 ms ease-out.

RAISES: type at full scale (from the fight poster); teal as a total commitment, never an accent (from the bitmap collage); one 12-column grid everything aligns to (from the oscilloscope); status never by colour alone (from the exposure sheet); rooms revealed in order along one axis (from Versailles).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
